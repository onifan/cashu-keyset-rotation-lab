Simulate a wallet input fee

Pick one currency unit and adjust the number of input proofs from each keyset. The fee depends on how many proofs you spend and the rate for each one.

How the fee is rounded

A rate of 100 ppk means 100 fee units per 1,000 input proofs. For example, two proofs at 100 ppk and one at 500 ppk add up to 700 ppk. Divide by 1,000 and round up once for the whole swap. In sat-denominated ecash, the result is 1 sat.
